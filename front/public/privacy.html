Spry privacy notice

Spry is an educational shared-meetings application operated by Mandzyuk Sviatoslav. Contact: mandzyuk.pn@ucu.edu.ua.

Sign-in information

Email/password and Google sign-in use AWS Cognito. Google access is limited to OpenID identity, email and basic profile information. Spry does not request access to your Gmail messages, contacts, Drive files or Google Cloud resources. Cognito stores the account identity and, for password accounts, manages the password; Spry's meeting API does not receive your Google password.

Browser session

The browser keeps sign-in tokens and an unfinished meeting draft in tab-local sessionStorage. A draft is restored only for the same Cognito identity. Explicit Sign out clears the local session and saved drafts. Do not use a shared browser session for sensitive information.

Shared meeting data

Meeting titles, times and attendee counts are stored in PostgreSQL on AWS. All signed-in users can view, create and permanently delete any meeting. There is no private ownership or automatic account linking. Do not enter sensitive personal information. Meetings hidden one hour after their end remain stored unless explicitly deleted; backups can retain earlier data after deletion.

Retention and service providers

Account information remains in Cognito until removed. Meeting records remain until deleted or the lab is deliberately retired. AWS services may keep operational/security logs and backups. Spry does not add advertising or analytics tracking or sell the identity information received from Google. Contact the operator to request account removal or discuss stored data; shared meetings are not attributed to individual accounts.
